MVP Scope — What Is In, What Is Out

The discipline of the MVP is what you cut, not what you build.

The one rule
The MVP proves exactly one thing: will a Web3 project pay $20 to get one verified on-chain user, and will that user complete the quest to earn $17? Everything else is noise until that loop closes with real money. If the answer is yes — build everything. If no — pivot before spending 12 months.

How the MVP Actually Works

The entire product in one loop — from project paying to user earning.

1
Project pays upfront into your Gnosis Safe multisig
You DM the project founder. They agree to pay $500 USDC for 25 verified Twitter followers. They send $500 USDC to your 2-of-3 Gnosis Safe address. You confirm receipt. That project's campaign is now funded. Takes 10 minutes, zero code.
Project sends USDC → gnosis.safe.address → you confirm in safe.global UI → campaign marked FUNDED in your DB
2
User arrives at Web3Cash, connects wallet
User opens web3cash.io (your Next.js app). Clicks Connect Wallet. MetaMask or WalletConnect pops up. User signs a message — no gas, just a signature proving they own the wallet. You store: wallet address, chain, timestamp. Account created. Done.
sign("Login to Web3Cash: " + nonce) → verify signature server-side → create user record
3
Sybil score checked — 2 signals only
Alchemy API call: how old is this wallet? How many transactions has it ever made? If wallet is less than 7 days old AND has fewer than 3 transactions — blocked from quests, sent to review. Takes about 500ms. Run this once at registration, cache the score.
alchemy.core.getAssetTransfers(wallet) → count txs → wallet.age = Date.now() - firstTxTimestamp
4
User sees quest feed — clicks a quest
Three quests on screen. Each shows: project name, what to do (follow @ProjectX on Twitter), reward amount in USDC, and how many spots remain. User clicks Start. You store a quest_attempt record with status PENDING.
5
User connects Twitter via OAuth — bot verifies follow
User clicks Connect Twitter. They're redirected to Twitter OAuth. They approve. Twitter sends back an access token. Your bot uses that token to call the Twitter API and check if the user follows the target account right now. If yes — verified. Stores: twitter_id → wallet_address mapping (one Twitter per wallet, enforced).
GET /2/users/:id/following → check if target_account_id in results → mark VERIFIED or FAILED
6
72-hour hold — unfollow detection
You do NOT pay out immediately. You schedule a re-check job 72 hours later (BullMQ delayed job). At 72 hours, your bot checks the follow again. Still following = payout released. Unfollowed = payout cancelled, user flagged. This one rule eliminates 90% of follow-farm fraud.
bullmq.add('recheck-follow', {completionId}, {delay: 72 * 60 * 60 * 1000})
7
USDC paid from Gnosis Safe to user wallet
At 72 hours, the re-check passed. Admin panel shows: "12 payouts ready to release." You (or a co-signer) approve the batch transaction in Gnosis Safe. USDC flows from the Safe to each user's wallet address. Transaction confirmed on Ethereum. User sees balance update. Real money, real blockchain, real user.
gnosis.safe.proposeTransaction(batch_payouts) → 2-of-3 signers approve → USDC sent onchain
8
Referral bonus credited to referrer
When the payout goes to the user, your backend checks: was this user referred by someone? If yes, 10% of the gross reward is added to the referrer's pending balance. Referral payouts batch with the next payout run. Simple math, simple DB query, no complexity.
Why manual payouts are OK at MVP scale
At 500 users and 3 projects, you might process 50–100 payouts per week. That takes 20 minutes in Gnosis Safe UI. An automated smart contract costs $20k to audit and 4 weeks to build. The math is clear: do it manually until you have 500+ weekly payouts, then automate. Circle USDC API can automate this in Week 10 when volume justifies it.

Getting Your First 3 Projects — The Exact Approach

How to find, pitch, and onboard your first paying clients.

The founder's job in weeks 1–4
While the developer is building, the founder is doing exactly this: talking to 30 Web3 project founders per week to find the 3 who will pay $500 USDC for your unbuilt product. The DM script below has worked. Use it.
Where to find projects
The exact sources — in priority order
1. Twitter/X Crypto Launchpads — search "launching soon" or "TGE" on Twitter. Projects about to launch need users desperately.

2. ETHGlobal hackathon winners — ethglobal.com/showcase — these are builders with projects but no users. They are technically sophisticated and understand why verified users matter.

3. CoinMarketCap "new" listings — projects that listed in the last 30 days. They have budget from their raise but no users. DM their official Twitter account.

4. DeFiLlama new protocols — defillama.com sorted by newest. Filter for under $1M TVL — these projects need users to grow.

5. Telegram alpha groups — join 20 alpha groups. The project founders promoting their own projects in these groups are your customers. DM them directly.
The DM script — copy this exactly
What to say when you reach out
Subject: Get 25 verified Twitter followers for your dApp — $500 USDC

"Hey [name], I'm building Web3Cash — a performance marketing platform for Web3. You pay per verified action (Twitter follow, wallet connect, deposit), not per click.

For your launch, I can get you 25 verified Twitter followers from real wallets with on-chain history — not bots. $500 USDC, money back if we don't deliver.

No contracts. No minimum commitment. I'll set it up manually and you'll see every completion with wallet addresses.

Interested? I can show you a quick demo tomorrow."

Key points: money-back guarantee removes risk. "Manually" signals you're not asking them to trust software they can't see. "Wallet addresses" proves it's real. Keep it short.
Objections and responses
What they will say and how to answer
"How do we know the follows are real?"
Every completer's wallet address is visible to you. Check their on-chain history on Etherscan yourself.

"What if people just unfollow after claiming?"
We hold the payout for 72 hours and re-check. Anyone who unfollows before 72 hours doesn't get paid. You only pay for genuine followers.

"$500 seems high for 25 follows."
A real Web3 user costs $15–$40 on Twitter ads, with no on-chain verification. We're at $20 per verified wallet. If you know a cheaper way to get verified wallet holders, take it.

"Can we see the platform first?"
Yes — here's a Loom recording of the current build. We launch to first partners in [date]. You'll be one of 3.
Project onboarding — the exact steps
What happens after they say yes
Step 1: Share your Gnosis Safe USDC address. Ask them to send $500 USDC to it.

Step 2: Confirm receipt in your Gnosis Safe UI. Screenshot it and send to them.

Step 3: You (or your dev) manually create their quest in the DB: project_name, twitter_handle_to_follow, reward_usdc = 17, max_completions = 25.

Step 4: Share the Airtable view with them. They can see completions in real time.

Step 5: After 72 hours, the first completions start passing. You process payouts weekly from Gnosis Safe.

Step 6: Send them a completion report after the campaign. This is what gets them to run a second campaign.
Getting Your First 500 Users

Where to find them, what to say, and how the referral engine amplifies everything.

01
Telegram crypto alpha groups
Join 20–30 active Telegram groups with "alpha," "airdrop," or "yield" in the name. Post one message: "Earn $10 USDC for following a Twitter account. Connect your wallet at web3cash.io. Real USDC, withdrawable instantly." Do not post the same message in every group on the same day — Telegram will ban your account. 2 groups per day.
02
The referral mechanic is your growth engine
Every user gets a referral link that earns them 10% of what their friends earn — forever. The moment someone earns $10, they immediately share their referral link in every crypto group they're in. This is free distribution. Make sure your referral tracking works perfectly from day 1 — this is not optional.
03
Partner with the project's own community
Each project you onboard has its own Telegram group and Discord server. Ask them to post your quest to their community. "Complete our Web3Cash quest to support us and earn $17 USDC." Their 5,000 community members are exactly your target user. This is co-marketing that costs nothing — the project wants their followers to follow their Twitter.
04
Twitter/X thread about your first payout
When the first real payout goes through, post an Etherscan screenshot on Twitter. "Just sent 12 users real USDC for completing quests on web3cash.io. Transaction proof: [etherscan link]. Quest live now." The on-chain proof is your credibility signal that no other platform can copy.
05
Airdrop hunter communities
airdropalert.com, earni.fi, the "airdrop" subreddit, and dedicated Discord servers track earning opportunities. Submit Web3Cash to these aggregators. Airdrop hunters are exactly your target user — they actively seek ways to earn crypto and they have real wallets with on-chain history (which helps your Sybil scoring).
06
Micro-influencers on Twitter — the 10% pitch
Find crypto Twitter accounts with 1,000–20,000 followers. DM them: "We'll give you a custom referral code. Every user you refer earns you 10% of their rewards — forever. No upfront commitment. Just tweet your link once." At $10 average reward, 100 referrals = $100/month passive income. That is a real pitch for a small creator.
